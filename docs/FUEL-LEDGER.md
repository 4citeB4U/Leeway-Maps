# Local fuel log and load estimates

Mount `mountFuelLedger({container, planner})` from `src/leeway/fuelLedger.js`. Both parameters are optional: the section appends to container, planner.root, or document.body in that order. The returned object has `root`, `getState()` and `destroy()`. `planner.getState().route.distanceM` supports an explicit copy-route-miles button. The module never changes the route, sends messages, books a load, calls a pricing service, or contacts anyone.

One vehicle log is stored at localStorage key `leeway.fuel-ledger.v1`; maximum 250 records and 100 KB decoded input. Browser storage failures are surfaced as memory-only operation. Individual fill deletion, fill-history deletion and full local-data deletion are available. Do not mix vehicles or odometer units. Data stays in this browser and can disappear when site storage is cleared. No cloud sync or account storage is implied.

Measured MPG requires a full starting tank and a later full tank. Distance is the odometer difference. Fuel consumed is the sum of all gallons added after the first full fill through the final full fill, including intermediate partial fills. The opening fill amount is excluded; trailing partial fills await a subsequent full fill. Multiple completed intervals use total distance divided by total gallons, not an unweighted mean. Every actual fill must be entered. Deleting a fill marks a discontinuity before the next remaining record, preventing the missing fuel from inflating MPG. The next full fill establishes a new measurement baseline. There is no automatic odometer, vehicle-tank or telematics integration.

Estimated range is max(0, current gallons − entered reserve gallons) × selected MPG. Tank capacity validates the entries. Current gallons remain a manual snapshot and do not automatically update from fill records or driving. Historical MPG does not guarantee future range.

Load distance is loaded miles + empty miles before pickup + return/reposition miles after delivery. Each segment should appear once. Fuel cost is distance / MPG × entered USD per US gallon. The total adds explicitly entered toll, labor, other operating-cost and tax/fee dollar allowances. Required fields remain blank until entered, with an explicit zero required for inapplicable costs. No jurisdiction-specific tax rates or deductions are supplied. Suggested counteroffer = total cost / (1 − target margin), rounded upward to the cent; margin is profit divided by revenue. Optional offered payment shows estimated profit/loss. Results omit anything the user has not included in the allowances and are labeled estimates.

Run `node --test src/leeway/fuelLedgerCore.test.mjs` for measurement boundaries, deletion gaps, record/storage validation, tank range and quote mathematics.
